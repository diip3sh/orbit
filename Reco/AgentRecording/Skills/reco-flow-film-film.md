# The two reels, measured

Both were measured frame by frame (6 frames a second), with how much of each 0.1 s step changes at 160×90: Vantae's
changes under one level in 1 % of its steps (median change 5.7), IrukaDark's in 22 % (median 2.9). A film of cards that
appear and wait is still in half of its steps.

## What to take

- The transitions are things happening: the line becomes the bar, the click dives into what it made, the key opens into
  the error, the selection brings its answer, the picture melts into the ground, the mark flies to its name.
- The camera is never still: it pushes, tracks the caret, pans along a bar, tilts. Holds are the end card and a line being
  read, and even then the ground turns.
- The pointer is on every action: a hand on buttons, an arrow dragging across text.
- Light everywhere: white bars and cards with soft shadows, the brand's colour on buttons and the ground's light, ink type.
- Their music is a beat (Vantae's house at about 126 BPM, IrukaDark's 176 BPM breaks with a filter sweep into its drop at
  the mark's swim). Reco's flow films keep quiet chords and a sound for each transition unless the user asks for a beat.
