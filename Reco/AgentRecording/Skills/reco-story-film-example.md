# A whole story film: the reference rebuilt

Lovable's film, rebuilt in 24 s and checked against its frames shot by shot. The photos, logo and moodboard tiles are
shapes here. It is in Lovable's theme over the `aurora`, because Lovable's colour is a gradient: take its structure and
timing, and your look from your brand (its theme, one of Reco's looks, that look's seams). Its surfaces are flat and it runs
at 30 fps; yours are glass, at 60. In your film the marks are
lifted (`ui` layers, tinted) and the answers are rebuilt as tiles and cards (`recipes.md`). Read it for how the parts fit:

| Scene | Beats | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| wide | 4 | The prompt box rises over the aurora as its light sweeps in; the hand rises to the mode pill and presses it as the scene ends. |
| macro | 3 | The same box at 3.6×, the camera drifting left, the hand already on the pill (its click is 0.55 s in). |
| menu | 4 | The menu pops from the pill at 1.5×; the highlight follows the hand from the first row to the second, which it presses. |
| voice | 7 | What the person says, typed big in the gradient, the line following its caret. |
| send | 6 | The box with the sentence and two attachments popping in; the hand presses send and the gradient washes across it. |
| thread | 8 | The message, "Thinking…" shimmering, the reply word by word as the thread scrolls up. |
| bento | 8 | Six tiles popping from the middle out, all swapping to a second variant on the same beat. |
| collage | 7 | Nine cards thrown out of a stack round the frame's edges. |
| want | 4 | The logo and a statement typed big together. |
| explore, chat, with | 5, 2, 2 | End words in the moving gradient. |
| logo | 8 | The lockup; the light goes out. |

Every duration is a whole number of 0.353 s beats, so the groove's drop lands on the voice's cut, its break goes under
the statement ("Want to explore"), the drums come back for "Explore", and they go out under the end words.
