# A whole film

The document Reco's own look-dev made of the reference: 20.2 s, four scenes, each cut on a beat with the same
object carried across, and the camera carrying the rest. Its rows and covers are lifted from Spotify's web player,
signed out, at a phone's viewport, and its logo from spotify.com's header; everything else is shapes and type.

1. The queue builds and scrolls; the dash under it grows into the pill as the list leaves. The pill is hovered,
   clicked and floods the frame, and irises back.
2. The pill drains to an outline, fills with listeners and splits into Invite and Leave. Invite is chosen and
   flies to where the next text starts, becoming a caret.
3. A song's name is typed big from that caret and clicked.
4. Its row flies in; the camera pans in on its plus, a check bursts out of it, and the camera pans back out into
   the queue, which scrolls away under the logo.

The playlist's rows start at the list's first item; `div:nth-of-type(n) > [data-encore-id=listRow]` is the n-th
row on that page. Another product's rows need their own selector from inspect_page.
