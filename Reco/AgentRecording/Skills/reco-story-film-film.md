## The ground

Black, with soft lights in the gradient. A light is blue where it's faint and warm only at its strongest middle, at a
corner. The light moves to a new place with every cut.

Over the film, 55–77 % of a frame is black and 10–25 % is lit. Three moments light more than half of it: the box at 3.6 s,
the moodboard, and the end words. The `aurora` field does all this, for a brand whose colour is a gradient as Lovable's is.

## The gradient

From blue `#5b6ff8` through violet `#a783f5`, pink `#f582e9` and red `#ed3c55` to orange `#ef6720`, cool end first. Where
it shows:

- the newest typed words;
- a voice line's caret, in its pink-red;
- "Thinking…";
- the box when sent, washed in its cool half: blue, violet, pink;
- the end words, running warm to cool and on.

The UI stays the product's own grey.

## The music

- **Tempo and key.** 170 BPM drum and bass in F♯: hats in eighths with sixteenth rolls, claps on 2 and 4, an 808 sub that
  slides on its hits. The intro is F♯ minor; from the drop it's F♯7add9 with Badd9.
- **Form.**
  - Intro (0–8.5 s) without drums.
  - The drop as the story starts (the box with the photos).
  - A break under "Ready to build it?" (33.5–37 s).
  - Drums out under the end words (43.6 s on), and a last hit.
- **Level.** −15.8 LUFS.
- **Cuts.** They land on its beats.
