# Story films and scores

> A third kind of launch film, after Lovable's chat launch: one person's request told through the product's own
> prompt and chat, its answers as moodboards, collages and files, kinetic type in the brand's gradient over a
> lit black ground, cut to a 170 BPM beat. It ships as a third skill, `reco-story-film`. With it, the score gets
> styles: every film used to get the same slow pads; now a film can have a beat measured from its reference.

## Why

On 2026-10-09 the user shared Lovable's launch film (`https://video.twimg.com/amplify_video/2107892925518807040/…/MiCbYepyUM_1G6rz.mp4`,
copy at `~/Movies/Reco/references/lovable-launch.mp4`) and asked for four things:

1. Learn from it and make a skill, next to the Spotify one (spec 0014).
2. Learn from its music: "currently we only have one sound, we should generate different ones".
3. Then make a video about Orca ADE, from the app.

What makes it good:

- **A story, not a tour.** One person (a sauna-hat maker) asks for help; every shot is the product answering her.
- **The product's own controls, close.** Macro shots of a real pill, menu and send button, each clicked by a hand.
- **The voice as type.** What she says is typed big, the newest words in the brand's gradient.
- **Answers as things.** A moodboard, a collage, cards, a PDF: never a screenshot of a page.
- **One ground.** Black with the brand's gradient light, smooth (no grain), coming in from a corner or ringing
  the middle; it changes place with every cut and drifts within a shot.
- **The gradient is the brand.** On new words, on "Thinking…", across a box when it's sent, through the end words.
- **Cut to the beat.** Cuts land on the track's beats (within ±40 ms at 0.8, 5.2, 19.37).

### The music, measured

From the soundtrack (`~/Movies/Reco/references/lovable-launch.mp4`, extracted to 48 kHz):

- **Tempo** 170 BPM: hi-hats 0.176 s apart (eighths), rolls at 0.088 s (sixteenths), claps every 0.70–0.72 s
  (beats 2 and 4). Bars are 1.41 s, as the chord analysis found.
- **Key** F♯. The intro is F♯ minor (F♯ A C♯, with D); from the drop it's F♯ dominant (F♯7 add 9: F♯ A♯ C♯ E G♯),
  with Badd9 / Bsus2 bars and a C♯m7 colour.
- **Form** Intro 0–8.5 s (6 bars): no kick or snare, sustained chords (harmonics 180–800 Hz), pitched vocal-like
  swoops. Drop at 8.5 s: drums, sub bass with downward pitch glides on its hits, chords. Break 33.5–37 s under
  "Ready to build it?": kick and sub out. Back in at 37.2 s. Outro from 43.6 s: drums out, chords (B → F♯) under the
  end words. A last hit at 48 s.
- **Levels** −15.8 LUFS integrated, LRA 8.3 LU, true peak +0.4 dBFS (a clipped master). Body bands (2 s means, dB):
  sub −20, kick band −21, low-mid −23, mid −31, presence −38, air −36; in the intro, sub −45 and kick band −44.
  Centroid 2.3–3.4 kHz in the body.

- An `aurora` ground, a brand `gradient` in the style, and the moves the film needs: kinetic and word reveals in the

- The `reco-story-film` skill with its references.
- An Orca ADE film made from the app.

## Approach

### Sound

- `MotionSound.style` (`ambient` default). The Spotify skill writes `house`, the story skill `groove`.
- A beat score is cues like everything else (kicks, snares, hats, bass, chords), so the cue sheet stays the cache key.
- **Grid.** A constant tempo near the style's (±3 %) and a phase fitted so the most cuts land within 25 ms of a beat.
- **Form** from the plan: intro until the first cut after two bars (the drop), a break under a scene of big type alone
  in the second half, the outro from the closing words on, a last hit on the logo.
- Effects stay, 6 dB lower under a beat.
- Beat styles finish at −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.

### Look and moves

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| `aurora` field | Black, the brand's gradient light from a corner or two, smooth, the middle dark; scene k takes setup k mod n, drifting |
| `style.gradient` | The brand's gradient, 2–4 colours; `aurora`, word tints, `shimmer` and `wash` take it |
| `kinetic` / `wordByWord` with a gradient | The newest words in the gradient for 0.8 s; kinetic's caret a thin bar; a kinetic line follows its caret |
| `shimmer` | The gradient running through a layer's pixels (text: its colour) while it shows |
| `wash` | A band of the gradient sweeping across a layer or group, screen-blended, then gone |
| `scatter` | A group's layers fly out from its middle to their places, turning, then drift |
| `show`, `hide` | A layer there from, or gone from, its start: contents that swap on a beat |

## Phases

1. Score styles (`groove`, `house`) with tests, checked against both references' tempo, form, bands and loudness.
2. Look and moves, with stills next to the reference's at the same moments (spec 0012's rule) before motion.
3. The skill and its references; the Spotify skill writes `house`.
4. Orca ADE from the app.

Engine changes it needed:

- **Marks from Simple Icons.** An asset whose address is an image (`.svg`, `.png`, …) is lifted from a page of that one
  `img`: opened as itself, an SVG is an XML document and the lift's script failed in it. A `ui` layer's `tint` draws its lift
  in one colour through its alpha, so a published black mark sits white on its tile.
- **Text off the frame** is a design-check finding (`DesignCheck.cutText`) unless the camera is 2× or closer on a control or
  the text is a voice line.
- **Contrast** is read against the nearest fill under the text in its own group: dark type on a light tile under a
  translucent chip was read against the black ground, and nested groups compared shapes from other tiles.
- A voice line is exempt from the safe area: it runs off to the left on purpose.
- Not fixed: 16-bit grey-and-alpha PNGs (ImageMagick's SVG renders) drew nothing as `image` layers in most scenes, the same
  file at 250 px in one; as 8-bit RGBA they draw. Agents only use lifts, which are 8-bit RGBA.
- **The aurora refitted.** Each light is an elongated Gaussian (`elongation`, `angle`), its middle at or past the frame's
  edge, fitted with the ramp's core stops (black to 0.14, the navy to 0.52, the gradient over 0.55–0.92) to the film's
  frames at 0.5, 42.8, 19.8, 24.5, 13, 3.6 and 45.3 s, UI masked, in OKLab. Mean colour error 0.011–0.034 (0.07–0.23
  before); black and lit shares within 4 points of the film's. The film's warm corners pass through a hot magenta its
  five-colour gradient lacks, so ours go pink to salmon there. A monochrome brand gets `#7a64ff #4f7cff #8fbcff #e4efff`.
  The navy is kept below the gradient's first colour: darker, the ramp dipped and drew a bright line where two lights met.
