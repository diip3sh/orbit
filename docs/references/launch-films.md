# Launch films: references for spec 0012's look

Collected 2026-10-07 after the user rejected the ported fields (Q2.1) and asked for the best
launch films to pick a direction from. Every link was checked through oEmbed (title and channel
returned). Looks were judged from YouTube storyboard frames; Vimeo items marked *poster* only from
their poster frame.

## What they share, against what Q2.1 built

The light and texture act on the content, not behind it: rim lights trace UI edges and type, light
reveals words, dither is applied to the objects themselves, and the camera moves through UI planes
with depth of field. Q2.1 put a moving shader behind unchanged layers, which is why it read as pasted.
