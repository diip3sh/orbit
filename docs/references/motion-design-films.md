# Motion design films: references for more transitions

Collected 2026-10-08, after the user found the engine limited to "two or three styles" (Raycast satin, light/dither
grounds, the Spotify Jam kit of spec 0014) and asked for more transitions at that film's quality, to pick which become
skills. Every link was checked (YouTube page or Vimeo oEmbed returned its title; X posts by their video file), and every
film was watched as contact sheets of a downloaded copy, 1–8 fps over its transitions. Copies are in the session
scratchpad only (`motionrefs*/`), not in the repo.

The Spotify Jam reference (LordyVisuals) is a remake: its YouTube copy credits the original,
[Spotify Jam Motion Design Concept 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TAyBFZhT590) by sutoxoriginals.

Grouped by family; a family is what one skill would teach. Timestamps are where the move is.
